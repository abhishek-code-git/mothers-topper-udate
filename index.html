Understand how Mother's Topper works for students and franchise partners.

Mother's Topper is a modern supplementary education platform for Classes 1–12 that combines AI-powered personalised practice with expert human mentorship through online learning and neighborhood learning centres.
We support students from Classes 1 to 12 across foundational learning (Classes 1–5), middle school concept building (Classes 6–8), secondary board preparation (Classes 9–10), and senior secondary subject mastery (Classes 11–12).
Our AI technology acts as an intelligent learning assistant. It diagnoses individual learning gaps, delivers step-by-step doubt resolution 24/7, and adapts question difficulty dynamically. It works alongside human teachers without replacing human care.
Mother's Topper offers both online learning and offline physical learning centres, as well as a hybrid format where students attend centre sessions and practice at home with AI tools.

Franchise Eligibility

Who Is the Right Fit?

Eligibility Criteria

Check whether your profile and location are ready for a franchise conversation.

Criterion Requirement
Minimum Qualifications Graduate degree, preferably in Education, Management, STEM, or Humanities. Prior teaching or academic administration experience is an advantage.
Space Requirement 300 to 600 sq. ft. commercial or semi-commercial premises. Well-lit, accessible, and ground or first floor preferred.
Profile Focus Women entrepreneurs, edupreneurs, existing coaching centre owners, or retired educators with a passion for early-childhood to K-12 learning.
Infrastructure Dedicated internet connectivity, power backup, and seating capacity for 15 to 25 students per batch.

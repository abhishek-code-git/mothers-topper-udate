Step-by-Step Onboarding

The Onboarding Roadmap

Every step ensures operational clarity, proper site selection, and successful student acquisition.

01

Submit Franchise Application

Fill out the online franchise enquiry form sharing your contact details, target city, background, and commercial space availability. Our franchise team logs your request for immediate territory screening.

02

Business Discussion & Model Consultation

Connect directly with our franchise director for a comprehensive consultation covering the academic ecosystem, technology platform, investment structure, and local market demand in your city.

03

Territory Evaluation & Space Approval

Evaluate proposed commercial space suitability, catchment demographics, school proximity, accessibility, and territory rights to confirm an optimal centre location.

04

Agreement & Turnkey Centre Setup

Formalise the franchise agreement. Receive detailed interior blueprints, smart seating layouts, branded signage specs, and IT network configuration standards.

05

Staff Training & Official Launch

Complete comprehensive management and mentor training. Deploy hyper-local digital and print launch campaigns, host diagnostic open house assessments, and officially open your centre.
